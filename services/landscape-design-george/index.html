Garden design built for local conditions

Designing a garden in George is different from designing one anywhere else. Our clay-rich soil, winter rain and coastal wind decide what will thrive and what will struggle. With over 20 years of horticulture experience on the Garden Route, we design with those conditions from day one — choosing plants, trees and materials that look good in year one and even better in year five.

We design for residential homes and commercial properties alike, from small courtyard makeovers to full estate gardens at Fancourt. Every design is paired with a practical irrigation plan and a realistic maintenance picture, so your garden stays as good as the drawing.

Landscape design questions we hear often

Do I get to see the design before work starts?

Yes — every project starts with a 3D landscape design so you can walk through your future garden on screen, adjust it with us, and approve it before construction begins.

Can you design a low-maintenance garden?

Absolutely. Low-maintenance garden design in George comes down to the right plant choices and smart irrigation — see our water-wise gardens service for the full approach.
