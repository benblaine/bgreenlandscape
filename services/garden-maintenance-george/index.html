Garden Maintenance in George

A garden is never finished — it grows. Our maintenance service keeps home and commercial gardens across George healthy through every season, from summer growth to the winter work that sets up a great spring.

Ask About Maintenance

Season-by-season garden care

Winter is the most important season for a great spring. While the lawn rests, we improve soil quality, plan new layouts and sort irrigation upgrades — the 'behind the scenes' work that makes September transformations possible.

Spring

New planting, feeding and irrigation start-up as the garden wakes up.

Summer

Growth management, pruning and water-wise irrigation scheduling.

Autumn

Cutting back, mulching and preparing beds for the winter rains.

Winter

Soil improvement, layout planning and irrigation upgrades while the garden rests.

Storm recovery & garden rescues

Garden Route weather can be rough — after the May storms we restored several Fancourt gardens to magazine quality. If your garden has taken a beating, we can bring it back and make it more resilient than before.
